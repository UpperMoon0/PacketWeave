# PacketWeave 0.1.5

- Add a shared, bounded outbound transfer scheduler for native packet integrations.
- Send at most one 4 KiB chunk per transfer before an authenticated receiver receipt, with at most two active transfers per connection and 32 globally across integrations.
- Cancel stalled transfers after ten seconds without a receipt; reject stale connections, incorrect receipts, duplicate transfer IDs and excess work without blocking game threads.
- Add regression coverage for slow receivers, gameplay task progress, receipt authentication, cancellation, and budget release after timeouts.

Integrations must use the authenticated connection object, send a receipt after admission or disk write, and cancel their transfers on disconnect. Existing dispatch-only helpers remain available and do not provide network backpressure.
